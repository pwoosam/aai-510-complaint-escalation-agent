Northstar Financial Services 
Customer Complaint Escalation Playbook

1. Billing & Transaction Errors

Scope: Unauthorized fees, billing disputes, duplicate charges, incorrect interest calculations, or transaction processing errors.

Internal Actions:
- Review transaction history and account records.
- Verify whether the issue was caused by a bank processing error.
- Branch managers may waive fees up to $100 when a bank error is confirmed.

Escalation Path:
- Billing Specialist → Billing Operations Manager → Director of Customer Operations

Response Timeline:
- Acknowledge within 24 hours.
- Provide resolution or status update within 5 business days.


2. Service Delays & Long Wait Times

Scope: Excessive call center wait times, delayed account processing, long branch queues, or delayed complaint handling.

Internal Actions:
- Review staffing and service records.
- Document location, date, and service channel involved.

Escalation Path:
- Branch Manager → Regional Operations Manager

Response Timeline:
- Acknowledge within 24 hours.
- Provide follow-up within 3 business days.


3. Unprofessional Staff Conduct

Scope: Complaints involving rude, disrespectful, or unprofessional employee behavior.

Internal Actions:
- Record employee name, branch, or agent ID when available.
- Review call recordings or customer interaction logs.
- Submit incident for management review.

Escalation Path:
- Branch Manager → Human Resources → Regional Director

Response Timeline:
- Immediate acknowledgment.
- Internal review completed within 5 business days.


4. Fraud & Identity Theft

Scope: Identity theft, account takeover, suspicious transactions, or unauthorized account activity.

Internal Actions:
- Flag account for fraud review.
- Verify customer identity.
- Open a fraud investigation case.

Escalation Path:
- Fraud Investigation Team → Fraud Operations Manager → Compliance Department

Response Timeline:
- Acknowledge within 24 hours.
- Provide investigation updates every 5 business days.


5. Product & Platform Defects

Scope: Mobile banking issues, website errors, login failures, ATM malfunctions, or digital banking outages.

Internal Actions:
- Collect screenshots, error codes, and affected services.
- Check for known incidents or outages.

Escalation Path:
- IT Support → Digital Banking Team → Engineering Manager

Response Timeline:
- Acknowledge within 24 hours.
- Provide status updates until resolution.


Customer Communication Standards

- Maintain a professional and empathetic tone.
- Clearly explain investigation steps and expected timelines.
- Document all customer interactions.
- Provide regular updates for unresolved cases.
- Escalate high-risk complaints immediately.
